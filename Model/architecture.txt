Input (string):
    - The user will provide input in the form of an explanation of topics covered
    in lecture materials. The input will be an audio recording that gets converted
    to text.
Concept extraction:
    - Identify claims, concepts, definitions, and relationships
    - Look for key vocabulary and keywords
Reasoning analysis
    - Evaluate logical connections, missing steps, misconceptions, and unsupported conclusions.
Knowledge Gap Identification
Targeted Feedback
    - Share what parts were missed
    - Bring to attention areas where they became vague which may be from a lack of
        understanding.
    - Ask questions for them to answer to help guide their "repair"

Extra important feature:
    - For feedback purposes, the model should be able to distinguish between information
    that was not covered in the explanation and concepts that were misunderstood.
    - If a concept was simply left out, the feedback should state what topic was
    missed and prompt the user to submit a supplemental explanation including
    that topic before giving the final grade.
