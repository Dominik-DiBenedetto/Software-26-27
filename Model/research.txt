        - Given a set of nodes N, and a set of labels L, a knowledge graph is a 
        subset of the cross product N × L × N. Each member of this set is 
        referred to as a triple.

    Curry, Brian James. “Building a Knowledge Graph: A Comprehensive End-to-End Guide Using Modern Tools.” Medium, 17 Jan. 2026, https://medium.com/@brian-curry-research/building-a-knowledge-graph-a-comprehensive-end-to-end-guide-using-modern-tools-e06fe8f3b368. Accessed 2 Nov. 2026.
        - A  structured way of organizing information as a network of real-world objects, 
        events, or concepts (called nodes) and the connections between them 
        (called edges). Unlike traditional tables, knowledge graphs
        link related ideas together to mimic human memory.

        Building a knowledge graph involves six major phases:
            - Data Acquisition & Preparation
            - Entity Extraction
            - Relationship Extraction
            - Entity Resolution & Linking
            - Graph Storage
            - Querying & Application Integration
